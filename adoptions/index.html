Adoptions Tracker

Jurisdictions around the world that have enacted, proposed, or developed policy language substantially similar to the models in this repository. Most entries predate ODP and serve as validation and source material — direct adoptions will be tracked here as they occur.

Status definitions

Enacted
Legally in force
Proposed
Formally introduced but not yet adopted
In Development
Active drafting underway; not yet introduced
Influenced
Substantially similar language enacted independently

Match types

Direct
Language from this repository was used or cited
Parallel
Similar language developed independently — validation and example

Parallel developments validate model language: when multiple jurisdictions independently reach similar provisions, it strengthens the case that the approach is sound and implementable.
